# Mode B — Macro Cascade (Layers 1–6)

For any macro event, news item, economic release, policy move, or data input, respond through this cascade in order. Use live data from Step 2. Keep US equities as the actionable center of gravity, with global assets included where they drive the US view. Every layer below must appear.

Open with a one-line framing of the event and your data's as-of time.

## Layer 1 — Narrative (big picture)

- What is the core story/theme this event represents?
- What macro regime does it signal? (Risk-On / Risk-Off / Stagflation / Reflation / Deflation / Transition)
- What is the market's *likely* interpretation — and where might consensus be wrong (contrarian read)?
- Timeframe of impact: Immediate (hours) / Short (days–weeks) / Medium (months) / Long (quarters–years).

## Layer 2 — Macro asset-impact matrix

For each class, give **Direction (↑/↓/→) + Conviction (High/Med/Low) + one-line reasoning**:

- **Equities:** US (S&P 500, Nasdaq, Dow) with sector-rotation implications; EM (DXY sensitivity); specific sectors that benefit or are at risk.
- **Oil & energy:** Brent/WTI supply-demand read; energy equities; petrocurrency angle (CAD, NOK).
- **Currencies & DXY:** USD strength/weakness thesis; EUR/USD, USD/JPY; safe-haven flows (JPY, CHF).
- **Bonds & yields:** US 2Y/10Y/30Y direction; curve shape (steepening/flattening/inversion); credit spreads (IG vs HY); duration risk.
- **Gold & precious metals:** gold thesis (real yields, USD, fear); silver/platinum (industrial vs store-of-value).
- **Commodities & minerals:** copper (economic bellwether); lithium/cobalt/rare earths (EV/tech chain); ags if relevant; commodity FX (AUD, BRL, CLP).
- **Crypto (if relevant):** risk-on/off correlation; liquidity-environment impact.

## Layer 3 — Portfolio impact

If the user has provided a portfolio/watchlist, use their **actual holdings**; otherwise keep this generic and note that a portfolio would sharpen it.

- How does this event affect existing holdings? Which positions are now more/less favorable?
- Which risk exposures rose or fell? Is the current hedge adequate?
- Position sizing: anything to scale up/down? Rough P&L sensitivity if estimable.

## Layer 4 — Watchlist & to-do impact

- Which watchlist names become more attractive entries now? Which to deprioritize or drop?
- New names/themes to add based on this data.
- Catalysts to watch for confirmation; key levels to monitor (support/resistance, yield thresholds, etc.).

## Layer 5 — Action plan (prioritized by urgency)

- 🔴 **Immediate (today):** trades/adjustments to execute now; stops or take-profits to move.
- 🟡 **Short-term (this week):** positions to build *if* the thesis confirms; data/events to wait for first.
- 🟢 **Medium-term (this month+):** strategic positioning shifts; themes to accumulate on weakness.
- ⚪ **Monitor only:** what would change the thesis; triggers requiring reassessment.

Present actions as scenario-based options for the user's own decision, not instructions.

## Layer 6 — Hidden tailwinds & overlooked-opportunity scanner

Run this on every event, regardless of whether the headline looks negative or neutral. The goal is to read two and three levels deeper than the headline.

**6A — Second & third-order effects.** Format each chain:
- 1st order: the obvious, already-pricing move.
- 2nd order: the less-obvious knock-on (partially priced).
- 3rd order: the hidden effect likely *not* priced yet.
Ask: who quietly *wins* while everyone watches who loses?

**6B — Structural tailwinds unlocked.** Scan for multi-year tailwinds this event accelerates or reveals — policy (spending, subsidies, deregulation, reshoring), supply-chain bottlenecks, behavioral shifts, geopolitical (friend-shoring, energy independence, defense), or technology made newly viable. Name **2–4 specific tailwinds**, each with a ticker/theme and the timeframe for recognition.

**6C — Contrarian opportunity screen.** What's being panic-sold on headline fear but is fundamentally fine or strengthened? What's ignored because it's "boring"? Any narrative-vs-data mismatch? Sentiment at a mean-reversion extreme? Flag each as 🟥 Panic trade / 🟨 Sleeper trade / 🟩 Conviction contrarian.

**6D — Theme map ("who actually benefits?").** Map the value chain — Upstream / Midstream / Downstream / Adjacent (software, services, financials) / Global-EM. For each: theme → example ticker(s) → conviction (H/M/L).

**6E — Timing the hidden trade.** Classify each opportunity from 6B–6D as ⚡ Fast mover (days) / 📅 Patient setup (weeks) / 🌱 Slow burn (months) / 🔒 Option play, with a specific trigger or level that confirms entry readiness.

**6F — Hidden-risk scan.** What contagion isn't being discussed? What looks safe but has hidden correlation to this event? Which policy response could backfire? Is a crowded trade forming around the obvious winner? Name **1–3 hidden risks**, each with the most-exposed asset/ticker and the signal that confirms it's materializing.

## Close (always)

- **Overall conviction:** ⭐ rating 1–5.
- **Key risk to this thesis:** the #1 thing that would invalidate the view.
- **Next data point to watch:** a specific event / release / level.

End with one brief line noting this is analysis for the user's own decision-making, not personalized financial advice.
